## About EasyWebinar

EasyWebinar is an automated and live webinar platform designed for course creators, marketers, coaches, and enterprises. Products include Automated Webinars, Live Webinars, EasyCast (live streaming), AI Webinar Funnel Builder, and Easy Suite (CRM + webinar platform).
